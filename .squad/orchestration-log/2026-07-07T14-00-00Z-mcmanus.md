## Outcome

**Fixed: `limit: 12` → `limit: 13`.** `forloop.first` renders the full-width `.post-card--featured` lead (`grid-column: 1 / -1`, its own row), which left **11** index cards — divisible by no multi-column regime, so the last row had a hole at 3-col (3·3·3·2) and 2-col (2·5+1). 13 total = 1 featured + **12** index; 12 is divisible by LCM(3,2)=6 and by 4, so rows fill completely at 3-col (4 rows), 2-col (6 rows), and the legacy >1200px 4-col regime. **Durable rule:** with a full-width featured lead + N index cards, N must be a multiple of the LCM of every active column count; for this layout index cards ∈ {6, 12, 18, …}, total `limit` = that + 1. Verified on a clean `_site_check` build served on a fresh port + fresh browser page: rowCounts `[3,3,3,3]` @1280 and `[2,2,2,2,2,2]` @800, featured spans the full container width at both.
